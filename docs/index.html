Reporting something harmful

If a listing, a message, a review or a person is abusive, misleading or a scam, report it and we will look at it.

  • A conversation or a person: open the chat and tap Report or block.
  • A listing: open the listing and tap Report or block at the bottom.
  • A landlord: open their profile and tap Report or block.

We aim to review every report within 24 hours and will remove content or suspend an account where our rules have been broken.

Blocking someone

From a chat, tap Report or block, then Block this person. They will no longer be able to message you and their conversation disappears from your list. To undo it, go to Profile, find them under Blocked people, and tap Unblock.

Never pay a deposit before viewing a room in person, and never send money to someone who refuses to meet. RentAPlace never asks you to pay us, and never handles your deposit.

Đừng bao giờ đóng tiền cọc trước khi xem phòng trực tiếp, và đừng chuyển tiền cho người từ chối gặp mặt. RentAPlace không bao giờ yêu cầu bạn trả tiền cho chúng tôi, và không bao giờ giữ tiền cọc của bạn.

Common questions

What does the verification seal mean?

A seal means our team has checked the landlord's identity documents and their right to let the property, and that the listing photos have been reviewed. A listing without a seal has not completed those checks.

How is my deposit protected?

By law your deposit must be placed in a government-backed protection scheme (DPS, mydeposits or TDS), and you must receive the protection certificate and prescribed information within 30 days. The app's deposit handbook explains exactly what to ask for, and how to claim between one and three times your deposit back if a landlord fails to protect it. RentAPlace never holds your deposit.

Why was my listing sent back?

Every listing is reviewed before it goes live. If it was rejected, the reason is shown on the listing in My listings. Fix what was raised, save the listing, and that re-submits it for review.

How do I become a landlord?

Go to Profile and choose I'm a landlord — I want to list a room. Listing is free: no commission and no hidden fees.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Delete my account. This permanently removes your profile, listings, messages and any documents you uploaded. It cannot be undone.

Which languages does the app support?

Vietnamese and English. The app follows your phone's language, and you can switch it from the Profile screen.
