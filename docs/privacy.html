RentAPlace

Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 August 2026

RentAPlace helps the Vietnamese community in the UK find rented rooms without being scammed. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

We never take your money. RentAPlace is not a letting agent and never holds a deposit. Deposits are paid directly to the landlord. We do not sell your data, we do not share it for advertising, and there is no tracking or analytics in the app.

Who we are

RentAPlace is operated from the United Kingdom. For any privacy question, or to ask for your data or its deletion, email support@rentaplace.uk. We aim to reply within 5 working days.

Identity documents

To earn a verification seal, a landlord uploads identity documents (such as a passport, BRP or UK driving licence), proof of the right to let, and optionally a deposit-protection certificate from a past tenancy.

  • These files are stored in access-controlled storage and are visible only to RentAPlace administrators reviewing them.
  • They are never shown to renters, never shown to other landlords, and never published.
  • Retention: we keep a document for as long as the landlord's account is active and the verification remains valid, and delete it within 90 days of the account being deleted or the verification being withdrawn.
  • You can ask us to delete a document at any time by emailing us. Withdrawing it removes the verification seal from your listings.

What we do not collect

  • Location. The app does not request or use your device location. The city and area shown on a listing are typed in by the landlord.
  • Analytics or tracking. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no cross-app or cross-site tracking of any kind.
  • Payment or financial information. We never process payments.
  • Your contacts, browsing history or search history.

Notifications

The app can schedule reminders on your own device — for example, to check that your deposit was protected within 30 days. These are generated locally on your phone. They are not push notifications and no notification content leaves your device.

Where your data is held

Data is stored on Supabase infrastructure in the United Kingdom (London region). Supabase acts as our data processor and does not use your data for its own purposes.

Legal basis

Under UK GDPR we rely on: contract, for the data needed to run your account and let you use the service; legitimate interests, for verifying landlords, moderating content and preventing fraud; and consent, for anything optional such as adding your phone number.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, to object to processing, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

You can delete your account yourself, from inside the app: Profile → Delete my account. This permanently removes your profile, your listings, your messages and your uploaded documents. It cannot be undone. Reviews you wrote about a landlord may remain, without your name attached, because they form part of that landlord's public trust record.

Children

RentAPlace is not intended for anyone under 16 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page and, for anything significant, tell you in the app.
